# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want this project to be a welcoming, constructive, and technically focused
community. Contributors must:

- Be respectful and assume good faith while discussing changes.
- Focus criticism on ideas, code, and behavior rather than people.
- Welcome different backgrounds, experience levels, and viewpoints.
- Keep private data, credentials, and security reports confidential.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats,
deliberate disruption, unwanted contact, publishing private information, and
any attempt to use this project to bypass access controls or misuse upstream
services.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, decline contributions, restrict
participation, or remove access when necessary to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's maintainer contact
options. Do not include sensitive account data, credentials, signed URLs, or
downloaded content in a report. Security vulnerabilities must follow
[`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).

Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith is not allowed.
